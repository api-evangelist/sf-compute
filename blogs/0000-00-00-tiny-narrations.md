SF Compute released a synthetic audio dataset containing 30,000 hours of story narrations based on TinyStories, synthesized using XTTS-v2. The 15TB dataset aims to support multimodal scaling research by pairing generated text with synthesized speech that exhibits natural variation in pronunciation. The project demonstrates that small audio models can achieve strong performance with distilled training data, similar to findings from the original TinyStories language model research.
